Beach activities

Dee Why is a long, wide beach backed by a lagoon wildlife refuge, with the biggest food and entertainment strip of any beach nearby.

  • Swim or sunbathe along the 1.8km stretch of sand, with a fenced kids' playground right behind it.
  • Take a surf lesson with Dee Why Salty Surf School, or swim laps in the 50m rock pool and kids' pool.
  • Walk the Dee Why Headland Walk, a cliff-top trail with ocean views heading towards North Curl Curl.

Attractions

See the scenic coastal highlights and nearby attractions around Dee Why.

  • Visit Dee Why Lagoon Wildlife Refuge, a bird sanctuary established in 1973.
  • Explore Stony Range Regional Botanical Gardens for spring floral displays and wild native orchids.
  • Head to Zone Bowling under Dee Why RSL for ten-pin bowling, bumper cars, and arcade games on a rainy day.

Food places

Discover the best restaurants and food spots near Dee Why Beach.

  • Masala Kitchen – modern Indian cooking with fresh, seasonal ingredients.
  • Beach Burrito – casual Mexican with outdoor courtyard seating, easy straight off the sand.
  • Dee Why RSL – seven bars and restaurants under one roof, from casual to formal.
