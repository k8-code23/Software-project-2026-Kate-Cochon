Beach activities

Manly is the Northern Beaches' biggest hub, offering a full harbour-to-ocean day out in one suburb.

  • Take a surf lesson at the home of Australia's first surfing contest (1964), with Manly Surf School.
  • Snorkel the sheltered cove at Shelly Beach, one of the best easy snorkel spots on the coast.
  • Hire a stand-up paddleboard at Cabbage Tree Bay, calm and beginner-friendly.

Attractions

See the scenic coastal highlights and nearby attractions around Manly.

  • Wander The Corso and Manly Markets, the pedestrian mall linking harbour to ocean.
  • Find the ancient Aboriginal rock engravings at Grotto Point, reached via a bushland track.
  • Walk all or part of the 10km Spit Bridge to Manly trail for harbourside views.

Food places

Discover the best restaurants and food spots near Manly Beach.

  • 4 Pines Manly Brewpub – local brewery on the harbour front, a Manly institution.
  • The Boathouse Shelly Beach – fish and chips with a front-row view of the cove.
  • Garfish Manly – seafood restaurant known for fresh, simply prepared fish.
