Beach activities

Freshwater sits inside the Manly-Freshwater World Surfing Reserve and is considered the birthplace of Australian surfing.

  • Surf the reserve break, one of the most historic surf spots in Australia.
  • Swim laps in the 50m saltwater ocean pool when the surf is too rough for beginners.
  • At low tide, rock-hop towards the Manly wormhole, a scenic tidal passage around the headland.

Attractions

See the scenic coastal highlights and nearby attractions around Freshwater.

  • Visit the Duke Kahanamoku Statue on the headland, marking Duke's historic 1915 surfing demonstration here.
  • Walk the 2km coastal boardwalk south to South Curl Curl Beach.
  • Browse Keel Surf & Supply for local surf and outdoor gear on the way in.

Discover the best restaurants and food spots near Freshwater Beach.

  • Shaka Café – laid-back breakfasts, acai bowls, and fish and chips.
  • Mrs Jones the Baker – artisan bread, pastries, cakes, and gelato for beach snacks to go.
