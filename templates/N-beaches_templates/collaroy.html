Beach activities

Collaroy is a relaxed Northern Beaches destination with space for swimming, surfing, walking, and family time. The beach is easy to explore on foot, while the rock pool and nearby coastal paths offer more ways to enjoy the water.

  • Swim or surf at Collaroy Beach, checking the flags and conditions before entering the water.
  • Take a walk along the beachfront and enjoy views towards Long Reef and Narrabeen.
  • Visit the Collaroy Rockpool for a quieter swim when ocean conditions are rough.
  • Bring a picnic, towel, sunscreen, and water for a simple day by the coast.

Attractions

Collaroy is a good base for gentle coastal walks and outdoor activities. The open beach, rock platform, and nearby lagoon provide different scenery across the day.

  • Walk south along the sand towards Narrabeen or north towards Long Reef.
  • Explore the paths and picnic areas around Narrabeen Lagoon, a short trip from the beach.
  • Look for seabirds and coastal wildlife around the dunes and rock platforms.
  • Watch the sunrise or sunset from the sand, promenade, or a nearby lookout.

Food places

There are several convenient places to eat around Collaroy and nearby Narrabeen. Choose a quick takeaway for the beach, a cafe breakfast, or a relaxed meal after a walk.

  • Visit a beachfront cafe for coffee, breakfast, or a light lunch.
  • Choose fish and chips, burgers, or takeaway food for an easy picnic by the sand.
  • Explore restaurants and cafes along Pittwater Road and in nearby Narrabeen.
  • Check opening hours and takeaway options before visiting, especially outside summer.
