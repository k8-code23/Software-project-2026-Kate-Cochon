Beach activities

Walking

Long Reef Headland Walk

The Long Reef Headland Walk is a scenic, easy 3 to 3.5-kilometre loop located on Sydney's Northern Beaches that hugs the dramatic coastline between Collaroy and Dee Why. A nice location for ocean views and a refreshing walk.

Surfing and swimming

Enjoy the beach for swimming or surfing, checking the beach flags and conditions first.
