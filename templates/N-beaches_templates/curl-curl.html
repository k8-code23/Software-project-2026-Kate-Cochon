Beach activities

North Curl Curl is quieter than its neighbours, with a dramatic rock pool and a boardwalk trail hugging the coast.

  • Swim or surf the beach break, rated among the best surfing spots on the Northern Beaches.
  • Take a dip in the North Curl Curl rock pool, set right on the headland with ocean views.
  • Walk the wooden boardwalk along the coastline towards Freshwater Beach (about 1.5 hours return).

Attractions

See the scenic coastal highlights and nearby attractions around North Curl Curl.

  • Follow the coastal nature walk from the surf club for sweeping views across to Manly.
  • Visit Curl Curl Lagoon, with its sand dunes and calm water, great for kids and dogs.
  • Let the dog off-leash at the grassy area behind the beach with lagoon access.

Food places

Discover the best restaurants and food spots near North Curl Curl Beach.

  • Emelia's Vegetarian Restaurant – a dedicated vegetarian menu, good for meat-free groups.
  • Split Coffee Espresso Bar – a sustainability-focused café for a morning coffee before the beach.
  • Muchacha Mexican Kitchen – modern Californian-Mexican food using sustainably raised meat.
