Curl Curl Beach

Discover the best activities, local food, and scenic sights at Curl Curl on Sydney's Northern Beaches.

Curl Curl Beach is a great place for families, surfers, and beach walkers. Use this guide to explore the top things to do, the best eats nearby, and the sights you won't want to miss.

Food to eat

Enjoy classic seaside snacks and nearby cafes for a tasty break.

  • Fish and chips from a local takeaway
  • Ice cream or gelato from a beachside kiosk
  • Cafe brunch with avocado toast and coffee
  • Fresh seafood and casual meals by the water

Sights to explore

See the scenic coastal highlights and nearby attractions around Collaroy.

  • Walk along the Collaroy promenade and rock shelf
  • Explore nearby rock pools at low tide
  • Visit the Collaroy Surf Life Saving Club
  • Enjoy the coastal parklands and ocean views

Visitor tips

  • Bring sunscreen, a hat, and water for a full day outdoors.
  • Check tide times before exploring rock pools.
  • Parking can fill up on weekends, so arrive early if you can.
  • Swim between the flags and obey lifeguard instructions.
