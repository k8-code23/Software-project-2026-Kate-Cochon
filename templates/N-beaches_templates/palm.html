Beach activities

Palm Beach is the Northern Beaches' furthest point, bordered by ocean, Pittwater, and Broken Bay.

  • Surf the northern end of the beach, away from the calmer southern pool.
  • Kayak the calm waters of Pittwater on a guided tour, a contrast to the open ocean side.
  • Hike the trail up to Barrenjoey Lighthouse for coastal views in three directions.

Attractions

See the scenic coastal highlights and nearby attractions around Palm Beach.

  • Visit Barrenjoey Lighthouse, a heritage-listed landmark reachable by trail or ferry.
  • Swim the protected Palm Beach Ocean Pool at the southern end for calmer water.
  • Join a Home and Away filming location tour around the headland.

Food places

Discover the best restaurants and food spots near Palm Beach.

  • The Boathouse Palm Beach – famous fish and chips right on the sand.
  • Barrenjoey House – upscale seafood dining with water views.
  • Jonah's – acclaimed fine dining just around the headland at Whale Beach.
