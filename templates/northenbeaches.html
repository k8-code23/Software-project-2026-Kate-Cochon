Sydney's Northern Beaches is a stunning, 30-kilometer coastal district in New South Wales.
Stretching from Manly in the south to Palm Beach in the north, it offers a laid-back, outdoor lifestyle, iconic surf spots, tranquil estuaries, and vibrant seaside village
